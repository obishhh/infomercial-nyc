New7

  • Find friends from your contacts

  • Your page got a tidy-up

    • Friends, Requests and Find sit in one line under your card.
    • Edit moved up top, next to Menu.

  • Your other shows, listed properly

    • Where you're up to and when the next one's out, at a glance.

  • The phone-in goes full width

    • Shorter, so more of On Air fits on screen.

  • The menu, sorted

    • Plus a link to this website.

Faster5

  • Changing channel is instant

    • Opening and closing pages too. No more fades.

  • Smoother scrolling

    • Even when On Air gets long.

  • The keyboard and the page move together

    • Post and Send stay right above the keys.

  • Quicker to open

    • Faces and posters show up sooner.

  • Sliders keep up with your thumb

Fixed5

  • Liking a phone-in answer won't close the app

  • A patchy connection won't send you back to setup

  • Likes stick, even mid-refresh

    • Mostly in search, Watching now and Find.
