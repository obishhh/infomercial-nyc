Infomercial · A TV rating and review app

Rate a show, review it, and say how far through you are. Every review remembers the place it was written from, so anything ahead of you stays scrambled until you catch up.

Something's broken, or I have a question

Email owenbishop364@gmail.com. Say what you were doing and what happened. It goes to a person, not a queue.

Why is a review scrambled?

Because it was written from further into the show than you have got. The scrambled screen says how many episodes away it is, never what it says. Catch up and it clears on its own. Tap it and you tune in early: being spoiled is your call.

The app thinks I'm in the wrong place

Open the show and move the position slider: tap the episode you are on, or drag to it. A thumb brushing it on the way past moves nothing, so a stray scroll can't mark six episodes watched. That one control is the whole truth about where you are, and everything else is worked out from it. It stops at the last episode that is out.

How do I find my friends?

You, on channel 3, then Find people, and type their handle. Friends are people who have added each other, so it shows as a request until they add you back. On Air is you and your friends, which is why it is quiet until you have some.

What's the front line?

In a watch party, it's the furthest anyone can talk about without spoiling somebody. It sits wherever the person furthest behind has got to. It isn't a leaderboard, and it never names who's behind.

What's a break?

The episode a party has agreed to stop at. Anyone in the party can move it, and anyone in it can ask their own friends in. A break says where everyone meant to stop. It never unlocks a review you haven't earned.

Can I rate individual episodes?

You can post about one, in words. Pips are for a season or for the whole show. A season takes a week, so being two episodes back is a fact about a calendar, not a score.

Can I change how it looks?

There is one look. Your colour is yours to pick: You, on channel 3, then Edit on your card. Sign out is under Menu, in the top corner of You.

Notifications

You, then Menu, then Notifications: a switch for each kind. A notification says who, which show and where. It never carries anything anybody wrote.

Somebody is being a pain

Hold down anything they wrote, then Block or Report. Or go to the foot of their channel, or to You, then Menu, then Block or report, and type their handle. Blocking works both ways: neither of you sees what the other writes, and neither can find or add the other. They are not told. A report comes to a person, who reads it within a day.

Delete something I posted

Hold it down, then Delete. It asks once. A reply somebody has answered leaves the word "deleted" in its place, so their answers still make sense.

Delete my account

You, then Menu, then Delete account. It asks once, and then your account and everything in it goes, permanently. If you can't get into the app, email owenbishop364@gmail.com and say which handle.

A show is missing

Tell us the title. The catalogue doesn't carry talk shows or soaps, and it skips any season over 120 episodes, because a daily strip isn't something you're "up to episode seven of".
