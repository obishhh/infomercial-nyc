Infomercial is a small app run by one person. Signing in means you agree to this page. It is short on purpose.

Who can use it

You need to be 13 or over, and old enough where you live to agree to this yourself.

Your account

One account each, and it is yours. Your handle is public and cannot be changed, so pick one you are happy to be known by, and not somebody else's name.

What you post

Your reviews, rankings, replies, phone-in answers, calls, the line on your card and your photo are yours. By posting them you let Infomercial store them and show them to other people using the app, for as long as your account is there. Delete your account and they go with it. You can also delete a post or a reply of your own whenever you like: hold it down, then Delete. If it was reported first, a copy of it stays with the report.

What isn't allowed

There is no tolerance here for objectionable content or for people who abuse other people. Do not post, in words or in a photo:

  • harassment, threats or bullying;
  • hate aimed at people for who they are;
  • sexual or graphically violent material;
  • anything illegal, or anybody's private details;
  • spam, advertising, or a pretence of being somebody else.

Do not try to break the app, get at other people's data, or collect what people have posted by machine.

What happens if you do

Anybody can block you, report you, or report something you posted: by holding it down, from the foot of your channel, or from Menu under Block or report. A report is read by a person within 24 hours. Whatever breaks these rules is taken down, and the account that posted it can be removed with it, without notice.

The listings

Shows and artwork come from TMDB and casts from TVmaze. They can be wrong or late. Posters and pictures belong to whoever made them.

No promises

Infomercial is provided as it is. It is new: things can break, and something you wrote could be lost. As far as the law allows, we are not liable for that, or for what other people post.

Leaving

You can delete your account whenever you like: You, then Menu, then Delete account.

Changes

If these terms change, the date at the top changes with them. Carrying on using Infomercial after that means agreeing to the new ones.
